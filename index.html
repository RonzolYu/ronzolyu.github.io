Research

Neural operators for inverse problems

I study how neural operators can recover subsurface structure from seismic data. My work on U-UNO combines a U-Net branch for local spatial features with a Fourier neural operator branch for global spectral dependencies.

Numerical approximation for nonlinear PDEs

I work on greedy algorithms for constructing approximation spaces for nonlinear PDEs. In the CGA project, I contribute to the manuscript and maintain reproducible numerical experiments.
